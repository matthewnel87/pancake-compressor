# Our promise

Pancake Compressor exists to be the best free, lightweight image compression and conversion app for fast websites. These commitments are part of the project, not marketing:

1. **Free, always.** No price, no paid tier, no "pro" features, no trial, no batch limits set by software.
2. **Open source, always.** The code is public under GPL-3.0-or-later. The licence makes sure that the app, and any version anyone else distributes, stays free and open.
3. **Your images stay yours.** The app processes images on your computer only. No uploads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
4. **No surprises.** No hidden network activity, no silent updates, no bundled extra software.
5. **Honesty first.** We say what has been tested and what has not, and we never claim the app is signed, audited or "100% safe" unless that is true and shown.
6. **Open to everyone.** Anyone may use it, study it, suggest ideas, contribute, fork it or build something new on the idea.

If a change would break any of these, it does not ship. If you ever think we have broken one, please say so publicly in [Discussions](../../discussions).
